Wide failure recovery and exactly reversible initial row scaling
2026-11-04; simplex2.0.22, LP contract0.4.6

Run from a packaged checkout (licensed Stata16+, Python3.10+, NumPy/SciPy):
 python3 tests/test_wide_scaling.py --stata /path/to/stata-mp --output /new/test-output
The output directory must be new. No private research paths, R packages or
network downloads are used. Source and fixture SHA256 values are recorded.

269 finite checks, grouped into three independent Stata sessions:
- 56 thin column-selection controls:49 full-rank constructions and7 invalid/zero
  controls, up to32 rows and10,000 columns, fixed columns, duplicates and ties.
  Selected original columns are checked for full rank with rational elimination.
- 68 multiplier controls:67 stored binary64 vectors plus1 missing-value control.
  Binary input includes subnormal values, extreme exponents, mixed ranges, zeros
  and empty rows. Fraction checks both forward exact scaling and reversal.
- 21 wide LPs:3 exact positive-coefficient seeds at127/128/129/256/1024/4090/4091
  columns. Rational primal and dual witnesses certify the original optimum6.
  18 must return the exact primal. The3 largest retain numerical-failure status
  at the separate standard-form quality bound n+2m<=4096.
- 124 public LP commands:62 inputs under none/row.124 required successes and
  118 required exact primals. Every returned solution is checked in original
  units, against the supplied tolerance and a1e-8 relative objective benchmark.
  The former c337 none/row failures now require exact reconstruction recovery.

The fixture refers to packaged precision_recovery.json indices and contains
hexadecimal binary64 multiplier controls. Source problems have strictly positive
original coefficients/RHS/cost; signed or zero helper controls are general
numerical tests, not negative-valued DEA observations.

Selection is a proposal, not a universal rank or feasibility oracle. At most two
real-column bases are proposed. Small matrices retain the established padded QR
path; above128 columns thin Householder work avoids padding up to n-by-n.
Original-factor solve, nonnegativity, original residual checks and the bounded
quality screen still decide acceptance. No original LP row is removed by this
new transition. Exponent and dimension limits remain documented in
PRECISION_RECOVERY.txt; no unlimited-size or universal-optimality claim is made.

Optional scaling(row)/lpscaling(row) now uses a power-of-two multiplier for each
row jointly with its RHS and another for the objective. If exact reversal or
finite-range checks fail, that transformation uses1. Default none is unchanged.
This initial representation change applies to the shared LP contract, including
simplex/ipm/auto and MILP nodes. It can change already accepted numerical results.
It is distinct from failure-only simplex recovery. A documented small near case
c191 row has a tiny error increase within tolerance, while4 near cases become
exact. Neither monotone precision improvement nor success for all LPs is promised.
Native IPM's internal algorithm is unchanged.

Keep the separate precision_recovery suite:396 exact-witness conditions require
396 available solutions and279 exact recoveries. The known e015 objective
boundary remains explicitly recorded; general linear dependence and unsupported degenerate supports
remain outside the bounded duplicate policy. See DUPLICATE_RECOVERY.txt.
